#### :rocket: ML Workflow Optimisation

At Relation, I focus on enabling rapid research iteration through the optimization of model, data, and benchmarking pipelines. This involves enhancing and maintaining scheduled regression testing pipelines using statistical divergence on training and inference metrics for foundation models. Additionally, I inform platform design on storage and caching solutions, recently benchmarking newly acquired NVIDIA DGX B200 nodes to ensure optimal performance.

#### :microscope: Novel Research

I have contributed to novel transformer tokenization alternatives for DNA language models. Our paper, **[PatchDNA: A Flexible and Biologically-Informed Alternative to Tokenization for DNA](https://openreview.net/forum?id=AFZeojzjoG)**, has been accepted at ICLR. This work proposes a biologically informed strategy using evolutionary conservation scores to guide 'patch' boundaries, outperforming current state-of-the-art benchmarks with significantly smaller models. I am also working on optimizing large-scale DNA-to-function models for V2G (Variant-to-Gene) mapping.

#### :wrench: Engineering and Tooling

To standardize practices and accelerate research iteration cycles, I led the migration of core repositories to multipackage UV workspaces. This engineering effort has streamlined our development process, making it easier to manage dependencies and collaborate across different components of our research stack.
